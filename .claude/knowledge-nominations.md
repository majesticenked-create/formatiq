## Pending Nominations
- [081326] Platform: `backdrop-filter` on an ancestor element creates a new containing block for any `position: fixed` descendant — same rule as `filter`/`transform`/`perspective`. A fixed-position overlay nested inside an element with `backdrop-filter` (e.g. `.site-header`'s scroll-blur effect) does not position relative to the viewport; it gets trapped relative to that ancestor's box instead. Fix: render the overlay via a portal (`createPortal` into `document.body`) rather than as a normal DOM child. Confirmed empirically in `components/MobileNav.tsx` (comment at lines 60-63) after two consecutive "mobile menu is positioned wrong" bug reports traced back to this exact cause. Source: T2 audit of mobile-responsiveness commit `e66fa62`, 081326.

- [081326] [KEYWORD] For a multi-tool utility site (104 tools, 7 categories, all client-side), the highest-confidence "quick win" tier is dev/finance/security-adjacent niche tools with a genuine trust angle (aes-encrypt-decrypt, iban-validator, totp-generator, jwt-decoder, wordpress-password-hash-generator, ip-subnet-calculator, bitwise-calculator) — these combine low SERP competition (no dominant aged incumbent) with a real commercial pull from the "does this send my data anywhere" concern, making the client-side/no-upload differentiator directly relevant to search intent rather than a generic trust badge. Generic head-term calculators/converters (percentage calculator, bmi calculator, currency converter, loan calculator, world clock) are the opposite: high volume but dominated by aged single-purpose or big-brand incumbents, so they're deprioritized regardless of the privacy angle since that angle isn't what those searchers care about. Source: keyword map expansion for formatiq.tools, 081326, cross-referencing full 104-tool registry against SERP composition for ~85 net-new keywords.
